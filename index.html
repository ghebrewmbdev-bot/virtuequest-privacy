VirtueQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

VirtueQuest is developed by Weegix Technology Solutions LLC ("Weegix," "we," "us"). This policy explains how VirtueQuest handles information.

Summary

VirtueQuest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app content and progress tracking stay on your own device.

What VirtueQuest Stores

VirtueQuest uses a local SQLite database stored only on your device to track:

  • Which lessons you have marked as read
  • Your current day streak

This information never leaves your device. Weegix has no access to it, no server receives it, and no account or sign-in is required to use the app.

Who VirtueQuest Is For

VirtueQuest is intended for use by parents and caregivers reading educational material, often together with their children. VirtueQuest is not directed at children under 13 as an independent user, and it is not designed to collect any information from children.

Third-Party Services

VirtueQuest does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. If a future version adds an in-app purchase, purchases would be processed entirely by Google Play Billing, and Weegix would never see or store payment details.

Children's Privacy

Because VirtueQuest collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children in violation of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or similar laws.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
